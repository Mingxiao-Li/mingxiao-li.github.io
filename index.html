Point of view

The thread running through my work is multimodal intelligence for scientific discovery.

I build generative and understanding models that connect vision, language, motion, and molecular structure—helping machines create, interpret, and reason across modalities.

Subject 001

Young Researcher · Shanghai AI Laboratory · AI for Science Center

My current work at Shanghai AI Laboratory centers on AI for Science discovery: molecular generation, unified molecular tokenization, and reasoning models for scientific problems. Alongside this, I continue to develop visual generation models, from controllable video synthesis to multimodal systems that connect language, motion, and dynamic worlds. Before joining Shanghai AI Lab, my research journey at KU Leuven was guided by Prof. Marie-Francine Moens and Prof. Tinne Tuytelaars. I care about ideas that are technically grounded, visually legible, and a little unexpected.
